About CSE Department

The Department of Computer Science and Engineering was established in 2001 with an initial intake of 45 students.


The department later expanded its B.E. capacity to 180 students in the academic year 2020–2021.


The PG programme in Computer Science began in 2006 with 18 seats.


The department received NBA accreditation in 2024 and was recognized as a Research Centre in 2023.
